info

Zone 0 was released in v0.2.0, along with making the game free to play. The purpose of this zone is to make the player ready for what's to come. It primarily takes place on a large, empty black abyss that existed before The First Difficulty(TFirD). The progression of Zone 0 mainly consists of regaining your senses, calmness, motivation, and eventually, yourself.

currencies

Memory

Memory is the first currency in Zone 0. Memory is used to buy most upgrades. This is a Zone 0 version of
Skill

Sensory

Sensory Power (or SP) is the second stat of Zone 0, also unlocked at upgrade #-30. This is the Zone 0 version of
Difficulty Power

Immemorial Time

Immemorial Time (or ImTime) is the second currency and third stat of Zone 0. It is the least used currency in Zone 0. When you buy upgrade #-26 (Patience), you start gaining Immemorial Time at a rate of 1/s. This is the Zone 0 version of
Immemorial time

Motivation

Motivation is the fourth stat. Motivation is a weird mix of Memory and Sensory Power and boosts Memory and Immemorial Time and is also the Zone 0 version of
tickspeed

calmness

Calmness is the zone0 version of Unfailability that you can get by taking a reset(rest) in zone0. Unfailability is often used in later upgrades.
